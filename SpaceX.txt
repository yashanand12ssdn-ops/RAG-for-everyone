SpaceX

Overview

Space Exploration Technologies Corporation (SpaceX) was founded in 2002 by Elon Musk. The company aims to reduce space transportation costs and enable humans to become a multi-planetary species.

Mission

Make life multiplanetary.

Major Rockets

Falcon 1
First privately developed liquid-fueled rocket to reach orbit.

Falcon 9
Reusable orbital rocket widely used for satellite launches.

Falcon Heavy
One of the world's most powerful operational rockets.

Starship
Fully reusable launch system designed for missions to Mars and the Moon.

Dragon Spacecraft

Cargo Dragon
Supplies cargo to the International Space Station.

Crew Dragon
Carries astronauts to the ISS under NASA's Commercial Crew Program.
