NVIDIA Corporation

Overview
NVIDIA is an American technology company founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem. The company is headquartered in Santa Clara, California. NVIDIA originally became famous for designing graphics processing units (GPUs) for gaming computers. Over time, it expanded into artificial intelligence, autonomous driving, robotics, cloud computing, and scientific computing.

Mission
NVIDIA's mission is to solve the world's most challenging computing problems through accelerated computing and artificial intelligence.

Products

DGX Systems
High-performance AI supercomputers designed for deep learning workloads.

H100 GPU
Built on Hopper architecture, optimized for training and inference of large language models.

Blackwell Architecture
Latest GPU architecture providing significant performance improvements for AI workloads.
